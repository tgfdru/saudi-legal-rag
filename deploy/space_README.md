# ⚖️ المساعد النظامي — Saudi Legal RAG

Ask an Arabic question about litigating against a government body. The app retrieves the governing
article(s) from 1,474 articles of 11 Saudi laws (official text, laws.boe.gov.sa) and answers with citations.

Code, notebook, dataset and report: **https://github.com/tgfdru/saudi-legal-rag**

Educational tool, not legal advice.
